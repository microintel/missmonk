01 — The Author's Two Rock Bottoms

Falling apart, twice.

Two moments broke him open — one physical, one financial. Both, eventually, rebuilt him.

First Rock Bottom

  • At age 20, hit by a drunk driver.
  • He died briefly, entered a coma — doctors doubted he'd walk again.
  • He recovered with the help of family and medical staff.

Second Rock Bottom

  • Years later, during the 2008 recession, his business lost income.
  • He couldn't pay his bills and fell deeply into debt.
  • He became severely depressed — this period felt harder than the accident, faced mostly alone.
02 — The Morning That Changed Everything

One run. One idea.

A friend suggested he go for a run. While listening to a personal development audio, one line stopped him cold:

"Your success rarely becomes greater than your personal development."

He wanted a successful life — but he wasn't becoming the kind of person who could create and maintain that success. That realization changed his thinking.

03 — Success Starts From Within

Your outer life reflects your inner life.

If you want Level 10 success, you need Level 10 skills, knowledge, mindset, habits, and discipline. Money, career, and relationships are often a mirror of beliefs, habits, and mindset.

04 — Finding Time

"I don't have time."

He wanted to improve every day but faced the excuse most people have. He weighed his options.

✕ Evening Too tired.
✕ Afternoon Too busy.
✓ Morning Nothing interrupts it — best choice.

A one-hour routine. Ten minutes each.

Six activities, ten minutes each — later known as the SAVERS routine.

06 — The Results

The dramatic turn.

After practicing this routine every morning, he became happier, less stressed, and more focused — and named the routine The Miracle Morning because of the change it brought.

↓ StressNoticeably decreased
↑ FocusMore energy, more clarity
2 monthsFor his business to recover

07 — It Helped Others Too

Word of mouth became a movement.

He shared the routine with coaching clients. Many reported similar improvements, and the idea spread through word of mouth and online communities — becoming a worldwide movement focused on daily personal development.

Key Lessons

What this means for everyone.

  1. Difficult times can become opportunities for growth.
  2. Your habits shape your future.
  3. Invest in yourself every day.
  4. Starting the day with intention can improve the rest of the day.
  5. Small daily improvements can lead to major life changes over time.
Simple Example

Two students, two mornings.

Student A

Wakes up, checks social media, rushes to college, and starts the day feeling stressed.

Student B

Wakes up 45 minutes earlier, reads for 10 minutes, exercises, plans the day, and reviews goals.

After several months, Student B is more disciplined, knowledgeable, and productive — not from one big change, but consistent daily habits.

Don't just chase better results.
Become a better version of yourself, every day.

The core message of The Miracle Morning.
