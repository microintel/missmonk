ideaToo many choices can make you too picky. This is the "shopping list effect."

before you swipe

What to do, what to avoid

✓ Do this

  • ✓Keep an open mind — this is a real way to meet real people.
  • ✓Still meet people offline too, not only through apps.
  • ✓Take time to actually know someone before judging them.
  • ✓Be willing to travel a bit for someone who really fits.
  • ✓Put in real effort — it shows you're serious.

✕ Avoid this

  • ✕Don't expect a "perfect" partner just because choices are endless.
  • ✕Don't keep swiping hoping someone better shows up next.
  • ✕Don't treat people like items on a shopping list.
  • ✕Don't give up on compromise — no one is flawless.
  • ✕Don't think you're "too old" to try — age isn't the blocker.
main lesson
More choices give you more hope — but real love still needs patience, not just more options.
