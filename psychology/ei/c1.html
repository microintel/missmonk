"All emotions are, in essence, impulses to act." — the root word for emotion means "to move"
7 signals from the chapter — tap to read each one
SIGNAL 01 Impulse
+
Emotion moves before thought does.
The word "emotion" comes from a root meaning "to move." Every feeling is the body already preparing to act — often before the mind finishes thinking it through.
SIGNAL 02 Misfire
+
Speed without a pause becomes a mistake.
Fear is built to respond first and check facts later — useful against real danger, risky when there is no danger at all.
SIGNAL 03 Wiring
+
Anger arms the hands. Fear arms the legs.
Every emotion sends a different set of instructions to the body — even happiness, which quiets the mind and rebuilds energy.
SIGNAL 04 Dual Core
+
One mind speaks in words. One speaks in tears.
The rational mind is slow and logical. The emotional mind is faster, less logical — and often more honest.
SIGNAL 05 Legacy Code
+
Built for tigers. Running in traffic.
Our fear and anger were shaped over hundreds of thousands of years — civilization is far too young to have rewritten them.
SIGNAL 06 Stack
+
Feeling was the foundation. Thinking was built on top.
The brainstem came first, then the emotional centers, and only much later the thinking brain — which is why feeling can still take the lead.
SIGNAL 07 Guardrails
+
Laws exist to fence in what instinct can't control.
Early moral codes tried to hold back powerful, ancient impulses — yet emotion still overrides reason more often than we'd like.
"Intelligence can come to nothing when the emotions hold sway."
Main lesson

We are not just a thinking species.
We are a feeling species first — and our oldest, fastest mind often decides before our newest, slowest mind gets a vote.

from "Emotional Intelligence" — Daniel Goleman, Chapter 1
