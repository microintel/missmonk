1.1 — What Makes 10X Investment?

Rule #1: A Large Market

The company ought to have a sizable market for its products to sell. Why do we require a broad market? If there is a small market, the company will not be able to grow beyond a point — there are numerous difficulties in scaling in a small market. If there is a sizable market, the company can simply continue selling what it's selling, in ever greater quantities.

✅ The General Rule
If a company is selling products worth Rs. 100 Cr., it should have a total market worth at least Rs. 5,000 Cr. — that's a 50x runway.
Company's Output: Rs. 100 Cr.
0 Total Market: Rs. 5,000 Cr.
👟 The Danger of a Small Market — Footwear Example
If the footwear industry is only a Rs. 500 Cr. market, even capturing 50% of it in a few years leaves the company at just Rs. 250 Cr. — a small number next to other huge markets.
Company's Output: Rs. 250 Cr.
0 Footwear Industry: Rs. 500 Cr.
💡 There are numerous difficulties in scaling in a small market. If there is a sizable market, the company can simply continue selling what they are selling and do so in great quantities.

1.2 — What Makes 100X Investment?

The Rare Leap From 10x to 100x

Searching for a 100x investment is incredibly hard, because 100x takes a lot of time and research. Thus, we'll start by considering a 10X investment — and for these, we can only try. Out of 10 successful investments, maybe 1 of them goes on to become 100x.

Each circle is a successful investment. Only one gets the rocket 🚀 — the odds of catching a genuine 100-bagger are low, which is exactly why the strategy is to keep finding 10x companies rather than hunt for 100x directly.

0 → 10x → 20x, then a long, uncertain arc up to 100x. The dot travels the path continuously — most of the journey is the easier 10x-at-a-time climb; the last stretch to 100x is rare and unpredictable.

🎯 We will be looking for 10X investments continuously over the next 7 to 8 years — because a 15-year ideal time frame is too long to make any reliable predictions. We will focus on identifying 10x companies, then see in a few years if it still has room to grow further.

Although it is rare for a company to grow 100 times its investment, when it does, a lot of wealth is created. 1–2 100x investments can make all the difference in our investment journey.

1.2 — What Makes 100X Investment?

In the Next 12–15 Years, We Want the Company To…

Don't get distracted due to short-term price movements — look for favorable valuations. Three requirements stack together over a long horizon:

📈

Grow EPS 30x–40x

Earnings per share compounding relentlessly over the full 12–15 year window — this is the engine of the 100x.

🛒

Bought Cheap

Entry valuation still matters at this scale — buying at the lower end of the band leaves room for re-rating on top of growth.

🌏

Huge Market, Still Growing

The addressable market must be large and still expanding — a ceiling anywhere along the way caps the multiple.

" Since these things are very difficult to identify on a 15-year timeline, we will stick to looking for 10x companies. And looking at its progress, we will decide or evaluate if it has the potential to grow 10x again — after growing 10x.
